## What this case establishes and what it does not

Both arms received the same dated market and evidence packets. The library arm also received
the complete orientation and access to its knowledge, history preparation, methods and guards.
Each date's raw decision was locked before its library decision, then the next date opened.
No final score was inspected before all 88 decisions were locked.

This is one non-blind retrospective case in an existing conversation with prior library and
market-result exposure. Internal reasoning was not budget-matched. Knowledge learned in the
library arm could carry into subsequent raw decisions. It is not an unseen holdout, a causal
estimate of library value, or a completed member of the requested twenty-model comparison.
It is also separate from the earlier personal case, which used a different interface and budget.

Evidence consists of selected Fed releases, Schwab surveys, CFTC futures positions and three
House actors' disclosures. Archive vintages and some availability dates remain unverified.
These sources do not cover all news, social behavior or officials' holdings. They must not be
reported as comprehensive information access.

## Findings from actual use

- The documented `pypfopt_hrp` example omitted required `linkage` and failed at library date
  index 0, turn 3. An explicit choice recovered within the same decision budget. This failure
  remains in the result. Both optional HRP backend examples now include an explicit choice;
  omission still triggers the library guard. This repair applies to future frozen runs.
- HRP often gave a high bond weight because of its risk-allocation objective. That is not by
  itself a numerical defect. The assistant chose whether to accept, adjust or reject it.
- `signal-reconciler` overstated the predictive meaning of its rule labels and incorrectly
  implied broad credibility weighting, entropy-based decision logic and execution enforcement.
  Its documentation and rationale wording are corrected without changing valid-input numerical rules. Invalid scores (including NaN), duplicate/unknown channels and invalid configuration now fail closed instead of producing a portfolio tilt.
- `stale=false` meant only within a broad retention limit. Weekly positions could be many
  weeks old. New packets expose the retention definition, a separate recency-review status,
  and the meaning of publication, reporting and filing ages. These review windows are declared
  heuristics, not a claim that historical availability has been verified.
- Survey populations must be distinguished. Publication age is not fieldwork age, and a
  recently filed annual report does not make its holdings or transactions recent.
- An explicit no-trade action in the model interface remains a follow-up design item;
  resubmitting weights is not necessarily identical to holding units through execution.

The remaining twenty-model work requires per-model tool/context qualification, the native
backend study runner and actual paired return runs. Template checks and review calls do not
count as completed models. Paid bulk API expenditure has not been approved.
