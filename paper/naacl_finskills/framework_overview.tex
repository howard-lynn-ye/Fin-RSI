\section{Framework: tools, routing, guards, memory, and dual-layer governed evolution}
\label{sec:framework}
We define research capability as the ability to choose appropriate quantitative methods,
interpret their outputs, and adapt future choices, discrete skill routing specifications, and
underlying feature operators based on realized outcomes without look-ahead leakage. Counting API
calls alone does not measure this capability. Our framework integrates progressive skill routing,
counterfactual execution guards, cross-episode associative memory, and dual-layer governed
financial recursive self-improvement (\texttt{Fin-RSI}) spanning \textbf{Skill-Space RSI} and
\textbf{Operator-Space RSI}.

\paragraph{Decision, memory, and dual-layer governed evolution cycle.}
Let $q_t$ be the task prompt, $\mathcal{D}_{\le t}$ the market data available at time $t$,
and $M_t$ the persistent memory state. A two-stage progressive router with point-in-time
(\texttt{as\_of}) filtering selects atomic skill cards that fit the context budget without
chunk fragmentation. The agent uses the hydrated skill specification and a readout of
$M_t$ to select a tool, execute it with chosen parameters, and produce an asset
allocation. When the trade clears at settlement $t+T$, the realized return updates
$M_t$, provided it passes all 37 verification checks. Across multi-generation research cycles,
\texttt{Fin-RSI} operates at two levels under SHA-256 harness locks: \textbf{Skill-Space RSI}
diagnoses umbrella-versus-leaf routing collisions and mutates frontmatter \texttt{TRIGGER}/\texttt{SKIP}
boundaries ($|d_k| \le 1{,}024$) and one-hop body cross-references under \texttt{SKILL\_HARNESS\_LOCK},
while \textbf{Operator-Space RSI} diagnoses mathematical bottlenecks in multimodal feature and
covariance operators and mutates candidate operators under \texttt{HARNESS\_LOCK} and a three-way
Pareto promotion criterion.

\paragraph{Complementary retrieval, memory, and dual-layer evolution timescales.}
Progressive routing operates within a single task by hydrating only the 1--2 complete
\texttt{SKILL.md} packages relevant to the query under a strict \texttt{as\_of} timestamp filter.
Persistent memory operates across trading episodes by storing sparse Kenyon-Cell readout updates
in an external module, while \texttt{Fin-RSI} operates across generational research cycles by
jointly evolving discrete skill routing boundaries (\textbf{Skill-Space RSI}) and the continuous
mathematical form of multimodal feature pooling, subspace shrinkage, and streaming covariance
adaptation (\textbf{Operator-Space RSI}) inside immutable evaluation harnesses.

\paragraph{Grounded feedback and anti-reward-hacking governance.}
When an episode concludes, the agent records the market context, the chosen tool, and the
realized log-wealth return. Only verified, settlement-cleared outcomes update memory. Negative returns
are retained as learning signals, while trades relying on look-ahead data, unbalanced panels, or
unadjusted prices are rejected by guards to prevent memory corruption. Similarly, during
\texttt{Fin-RSI} evolution, any discrete skill mutation that violates \texttt{SKILL\_HARNESS\_LOCK},
exceeds the 1{,}024-character description cap, or leaves thin-margin routing collisions ($<15\%$),
and any operator mutation that alters \texttt{HARNESS\_LOCK}, triggers a counterfactual guard
violation, or regresses on sparse small-cap or crisis slices, is automatically rejected.
