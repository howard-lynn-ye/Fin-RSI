\section{Supporting audit and validation studies}
\label{app:audit}
This appendix details the execution and feedback validation checks, defect recall
benchmarks, and empirical audits across model tiers.

\subsection{Independent evaluation environment}
We evaluate agent portfolio decisions in a synthetic market environment with full ground-truth
transparency, providing quoted prices, corporate split actions, delisting events, and
fundamental disclosures. A submitted strategy script returns portfolio weights, alongside
a written report stating its expected Sharpe ratio. Session-$t$ portfolio weights must use
only information available by the close of session $t-1$.

An independent oracle evaluator verifies submissions by perturbing future data, checking
for post-delisting exposure, and recalculating performance under fixed transaction costs.
Oracle grades are generated after the submission receipt is saved and are never exposed
to the agent during its decision turns.

This oracle separates genuine investment skill from data leakage. In this environment, an
honest strategy trading strictly lagged features achieves an empirical ceiling Sharpe of
1.63. In contrast, look-ahead joins (e.g., using current-day closing prices before the
close) produce inflated paper Sharpe ratios up to 71.5. Abnormally high backtest performance
thus serves as an unambiguous signature of look-ahead leakage.

\subsection{Validation benchmarks}
\paragraph{Defect detection benchmark.}
We evaluate 13 core research guards against 12 planted quantitative defect families,
including look-ahead feature joins, unadjusted stock split prices, future earnings
disclosure leaks, and survivorship bias. Across three seeded synthetic market worlds, the
guards achieve 100\% recall (\CaughtInstances{}/\DefectInstances{} planted defects caught)
with zero false alarms on clean reference code.

Without tools, Opus exhibited a reporting gap of \OpusBase, which decreased to \OpusLibrary{}

\paragraph{Social sentiment and multi-source corpus verification.}
Across our consolidated \CorpusTotalRecords{}-record (\CorpusTotalShort{}, \CorpusTotalSizeGB{}\,GB)
cross-market financial corpus (\texttt{shards\_v4}: \CorpusGubaRecords{} EastMoney Guba posts across
\CorpusGubaTickers{} tickers, \CorpusAnnounceRecords{} A-share corporate disclosures across
\CorpusAnnounceTickers{} tickers, \CorpusXueqiuRecords{} Xueqiu longitudinal posts across
\CorpusXueqiuTickers{} tickers, and \CorpusStockTwitsRecords{} US StockTwits messages across
\CorpusStockTwitsTickers{} tickers) and \KOLTotalAccounts{} verified KOL profiles (\KOLCNCount{} CN,
\KOLUSCount{} US; \KOLPredRows{} timestamped predictions over \KOLTradingDates{} trading dates),
naive follower-weighted sentiment exhibited negative out-of-sample rank correlation with subsequent
asset returns ($\text{Rank IC} = \KOLNaiveDailyIC{}$): blindly following raw social volume lost money.
By auditing each account's strictly lagged prediction accuracy and applying point-in-time Bayesian
credibility gating (including sign-flipping persistently anti-predictive accounts), out-of-sample
Daily Rank IC flipped to $\KOLGatedDailyIC{}$ ($\Delta = \KOLPairedDiffIC{}$, block-bootstrap $95\%$ CI
$[+0.0295, +0.0480]$) and reached $\KOLRollingDailyIC{}$ under 60-day rolling adaptive recalibration.
This confirms that unstructured text signals require empirical track-record verification before
driving investment decisions.

\paragraph{Skill-Space RSI four-generation evolution audit (\texttt{SKILL\_HARNESS\_LOCK}).}
Before evolving continuous alpha operators, an agent must reliably route domain queries across an expanding skill catalog without umbrella-versus-leaf collisions. Expanding \textsc{fin-skills} from $34$ Wave-1 core routers (\texttt{2026-09-08}) to $\SkillRSITotalCatalog{}$ skills across $15$ plugins (\texttt{2026-09-09}) introduced $95$ fine-grained leaf sub-skills whose positive \texttt{TRIGGER} vocabulary overlapped with parent domain routers (e.g., \texttt{perpetuals-and-funding} vs.\ \texttt{crypto-data-and-execution}, \texttt{portfolio-optimizers} vs.\ \texttt{portfolio-and-risk}, and \texttt{hong-kong-\allowbreak markets}/\allowbreak\texttt{korea-taiwan-\allowbreak markets} vs.\ \texttt{asia-pacific-markets}). Furthermore, because the read-only lexical router (\texttt{eval\_triggers.py}) extracts \texttt{[a-z]\allowbreak[a-z0-9\_.\textbackslash -]\{1,\}} tokens and treats all text after the first uppercase \texttt{SKIP} as negative vocabulary ($-\lambda_{\text{skip}}\operatorname{idf}(t)$ with $\lambda_{\text{skip}}=0.6$), hyphenated compounds (\texttt{moving-average}, \texttt{mean-variance}) failed to match space-separated user queries.

Under \texttt{SKILL\_HARNESS\_LOCK.json} (which locks SHA-256 digests of \texttt{evals/\allowbreak queries.jsonl}, \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak eval\_\allowbreak triggers.py}, \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak eval\_\allowbreak blind.py}, and \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak validate.py}), \textbf{Skill-Space RSI} evolves $\SkillRSIEvolvedCount{}$ skill specifications in-place subject to the strict character budget $|d_k| \le 1{,}024$ (observed maximum $\SkillRSIMaxDescLen{}$ characters) and relative margin floor $\Delta m(q) \ge 0.15$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:skill_rsi_margin}). \paragraph{Operator-Space RSI four-generation evolution audit (\texttt{HARNESS\_LOCK}).}
To evaluate whether a financial research agent can autonomously improve its underlying multimodal
pooling, shrinkage, and covariance-adaptation operators without falling into backtest overfitting
or reward hacking, we audit four evolutionary generations across $M=5$ disjoint random seeds
($20260923\text{--}20260927$) on the full \PanelBalancedRows{}-row two-dimensional
company$\times$year balanced panel ($n = \RSIEvalReturnObs{} / \PanelBalancedRows{}$ evaluated
out-of-sample return observations across $7$ equity boards, $2018\text{--}2026$). Prior to each
