\paragraph{Knowing the call is what helps.}
Pooled over \FixModels{} models (Table~\ref{tab:results}) and \FixArm{} episodes per arm, F0 was
valid in \FixFZeroValid{}
episodes (\FixFZeroPct\%), F1 in \FixFOneValid{} (\FixFOnePct\%) and F2 in \FixFTwoValid{}
(\FixFTwoPct\%); F2 did better than F0 in \FixFTwoMinusFZeroBetter{} models and worse in none.
With the interface alone, models imported \texttt{fin\_skills} in \FixFOneUsesImportsFinSkills{}
of \FixFOnePrograms{} programs but mostly wrapped a rule of their own:
\FixFOneUsesSignalPositions{} programs passed their own states to \texttt{signal\_positions()}
and \FixFOneUsesPositions{} called \texttt{positions()}. Documenting each rule did not change
that: F3 was valid in \FixFThreeValid{} episodes, as F1, and only \FixFThreeLastCalls{} ended on
a library call. Asking the model did: \texttt{route()} returned the task's rule in
\FixRouteRight{} of \FixArm{} F4 episodes, nothing in \FixRouteNone{} and a wrong rule in
\FixRouteWrong{}. F4 was valid in \FixFFourValid{} (\FixFFourPct\%), \FixFFourMinusFTwo{} points
from F2 and \FixFFourMinusFZero{} over F0, better than F0 in \FixFFourMinusFZeroBetter{} models
and worse in none. Of the \FixFFourLastCalls{} F4 episodes whose last program called the library,
\FixFFourLastCallsRan{} ran and \FixFFourLastCallsValid{} were valid, against
\FixFFourLastOwnValid{} of the \FixFFourLastOwn{} whose last program did not.

\paragraph{The gain is in the larger models.}
The \FixLargeModels{} models of \FixSizeCut{} parameters or more named the right rule in all
\FixLargeRouteRight{} of their F4 episodes and each did better than in F0: \FixLargeFZeroPct\%
in F0, \FixLargeFOnePct\% in F1, \FixLargeFTwoPct\% in F2 and \FixLargeFFourPct\% in F4. The best,
\texttt{\FixFFourBestModel}, was valid in all \FixFFourBest{} F2 and F4 episodes. The
\FixSmallModels{} smaller models never exceeded \FixSmallMaxPct\%: after \FixSmallFFourTold{} right
routes their last program called \texttt{positions()} in \FixSmallFFourToldCalls, and in
\FixSmallFFourToldNoProgram{} no program ran at all; none of the \FixRouteWrong{} wrong routes gave
a valid episode.

\paragraph{What the checks see.}
Of the programs that ran, \FixFZeroFailedExecutionLag{} of \FixFZeroRan{} in F0 traded on a
close they read and \FixFZeroFailedWarmupRowsZero{} held positions in warm-up rows; in F4,
\FixFFourFailedExecutionLag{} and \FixFFourFailedWarmupRowsZero{} of \FixFFourRan. Final programs
that passed all six checks but were wrong numbered \FixFZeroAcceptedWrong, \FixFOneAcceptedWrong,
\FixFTwoAcceptedWrong, \FixFThreeAcceptedWrong{} and \FixFFourAcceptedWrong{} in F0--F4; an
earlier run of F0 judged with the first three checks accepted \FixEarlierFZeroAcceptedWrong. No
property check sees a program that computes another rule or trades one row late. Models rarely
fixed what the new checks flagged: with an attempt left, \FixFZeroFlaggedThenValid{} of
\FixFZeroFlaggedRetry{} such episodes ended valid in F0 and \FixFFourFlaggedThenValid{} of
\FixFFourFlaggedRetry{} in F4.

\paragraph{Feedback and self-improvement without the library.}
In the study fixed on 8~November, out of \FrozenArm{} episodes per arm, \texttt{Repeat} was
valid in \FrozenRepeat, \texttt{Check} in \FrozenCheck{} and \texttt{Explain} in
\FrozenExplain: \texttt{Check} minus \texttt{Repeat} \FrozenCheckRepeat{} points and
\texttt{Explain} minus \texttt{Check} \FrozenExplainCheck. Out of \SNoLibEpisodes{} episodes on
the \SNoLibModels{} core models, \texttt{NoImprove} was valid in \SNoLibNoImprove{} and the
other methods in \SNoLibSelfDebug{} (\texttt{SelfDebug}) to \SNoLibCheckFeedback{}
(\texttt{CheckFeedback}, \texttt{Evolve}); every gain came from the two core models that
\texttt{NoImprove} already solved sometimes.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
We revised the library three times after seeing results on these six held-out tasks (each run
is released): the new checks, the factor reading and \texttt{route()} answer what those runs
showed, and every arm was rerun on the last revision. The tasks therefore no longer test a library fixed in advance; a
frozen library on unseen tasks is the next step. The tasks are three position rules on synthetic
panels, all among the library's own, and public. No experiment measures returns. The library
study ran on a cluster whose kernel lacks Landlock: programs could not open sockets or start
processes, but file access was not confined, so we scanned every program and found none that
touches files, the network or other processes (\FixScanNotPython{} recorded program texts did
not parse and could not run). All models are open-weight, at most 32B, with at most
\FixOutputCap{} generated tokens per reply (\StudyOutputCap{} in the earlier study).
