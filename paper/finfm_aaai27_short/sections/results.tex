\paragraph{The library goes unused.}
One library call solves every held-out task, but the library arms almost never make it:
across all models \ResLibCallsRan{} programs that called the library ran, and \ResValidWithLib{}
episodes were valid with a library call. \ResZeroP{} models wrote no valid program in any of
their \StudyPEpisodes{} episodes; the most was \ResBestP{} (\texttt{\ResBestPModel}). The visible
checks let many wrong programs through: \ResQwenAcceptedWrong{} of
\texttt{qwen-coder-32b}'s \ResQwenAccepted{} accepted episodes were wrong. Per model,
\texttt{Explain} minus \texttt{Check} ranged from 0 to +\ResExplainCheckMax{} points and was
never negative, \texttt{Check} minus \texttt{Repeat} from \ResCheckRepeatMin{} to
+\ResCheckRepeatMax{}, and the library under \texttt{Explain} from \ResLibExplainMin{} to
+\ResLibExplainMax{}.

\paragraph{Track E measures the interface text.}
The library left both \texttt{qwen-coder} models unchanged within 1\%
(\ResEQwenCoderBigNo{} to \ResEQwenCoderBigLib\%, \ResEQwenCoderMidNo{} to
\ResEQwenCoderMidLib\%), but \ResELibLower{} of the \ResModelsDone{} models lost accuracy,
\texttt{\ResEWorstModel} the most (\ResEWorstNo{} to \ResEWorstLib\%). The loss is one of format:
after the interface text, smaller models answered in prose (\texttt{\ResEWorstModel}:
\ResEWorstNoProgNo{} to \ResEWorstNoProgLib{} episodes without a program). No valid answer, in
any model, called the library.

\paragraph{Self-improvement.}
In track S only \texttt{qwen-coder-32b} wrote valid programs (\ResSQwen{} of \ResSEpisodes{}
episodes; the other core models \ResSOthers). Out of 18 episodes without and with the library,
\texttt{NoImprove} had \ResSQwenNoImprove{}, \texttt{PlaybookLearning} \ResSQwenPlaybook{},
\texttt{Evolve} \ResSQwenEvolve{}, \texttt{CheckFeedback} \ResSQwenCheckFeedback{},
\texttt{Reflexion} \ResSQwenReflexion{}, \texttt{SelfDebug} \ResSQwenSelfDebug{} and
\texttt{Team} \ResSQwenTeam{}.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The study covers three position rules on synthetic panels, all among the library's own, and
public questions the library does not cover; the newer tasks and the real panel are released
but not evaluated here. All tasks are public, so a model may have seen them. No track measures
returns, and we make no claim about them. The checks reject only the failures they were
written for. We moved track E's reported endpoint from exact matching to 1\% after the runs,
because of the label defect; exact results are released with the rest. The frozen source had
three defects that the release fixes: \texttt{Evolve} chose the development failures it showed
its proposer by the hidden grade; without the library, the \texttt{PlaybookLearning} proposer
saw library hints in \FactLeakPrompts{} of \FactLeakOf{} development prompts (in no test
prompt); and a reply fenced as \texttt{Python} rather than \texttt{python} ran whole as code.
All models are open-weight, under one attempt budget and at most \StudyOutputCap{} generated
tokens per call, which cuts off long reasoning.
