\paragraph{Limitations.}
Most episodes use synthetic panels with a fixed set of pitfalls; the real panel is one window
of four stocks with forward splits only and no dividends. The checks reject only the failures
they were written for, hence the accepted-but-wrong rate. All models are open-weight, under
one attempt budget.
